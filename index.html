About Our Free Company

Sector 7 was founded with a simple mission: to create a welcoming community where adventurers of all skill levels can thrive together. We believe that the true strength of any Free Company lies not just in individual prowess, but in the bonds we forge and the memories we create.

Our members participate in all aspects of Eorzean life, from challenging high-end raids and dungeons to casual social events, crafting, and roleplay. We pride ourselves on maintaining a drama-free environment where everyone feels valued and supported.

With over 40 active members and growing, we've built a reputation for excellence while never losing sight of what matters most: having fun together. Our experienced leaders and officers are always available to help new members find their footing and veterans reach new heights.
