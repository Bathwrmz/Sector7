About Sector 7

A welcoming Free Company built around respect, helpfulness, and enjoying the game without pressure.

Who we are

We’re a casual, community-first FC on Dynamis/Kraken. We keep it friendly, we keep it chill, and we help each other out — from questions and dungeons to maps and laughs.

What to expect

No strict gaming requirements. Jump in when you can, be respectful, and enjoy the ride.

Our Mission

Simple values that keep the FC welcoming and fun.

Recruitment

We’re actively recruiting. If you want a friendly FC with no strict requirements, you’ll fit right in.

• Players who want a relaxed community
