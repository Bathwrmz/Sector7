About Sector 7

Sector 7 (EXL7D) is a casual Free Company on Dynamis / Kraken built around being helpful, staying chill, and having fun together. Whether you’re brand new, returning, or a long-time player — you’ve got a place here.

What you’ll find here

  • Friendly people who answer questions
  • Low-pressure group activities
  • A community that respects real life
  • No strict gaming requirements

Our Mission

Build a relaxed, welcoming home for players on Kraken — where it’s easy to find people to run content with, learn at your pace, and enjoy the game without pressure.

Be Helpful

Answer questions, share tips, and help each other grow.

Keep it Casual

Real life comes first. Play when you can.

Have Fun

Laugh, learn, and enjoy the journey—wins and wipes.
